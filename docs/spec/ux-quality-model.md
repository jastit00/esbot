## 1. Intuitive Operation

**Quality Goal**  
The user is able to handle ESBot without help or further training.

**ISO 25010 Classification**  
- Usability: Appropriateness Recognizability  
- Usability: Learnability  

**Measurable Quality Criteria**  
- At least 90% of first-time users complete a core task without assistance  
- Time to first meaningful interaction is at most 60 seconds  

**Verification Method**  
- Usability testing with using the thinking-aloud technique  
- Cognitive walkthrough focusing on first-time user interaction  

---

## 2. Helpfulness / Efficiency

**Quality Goal**  
ESBot brings advantage in learning such as increasing the productivity and speed of learning.

**ISO 25010 Classification**  
- Functional Suitability: Functional Appropriateness  
- Usability: Operability  

**Measurable Quality Criteria**   
- At least 85% of users answer follow-up questions correctly after using ESBot  
- ...

**Verification Method**  
- Controlled experiment comparing ESBot with traditional learning methods  
- User Experience Questionnaire (UEQ) with focus on efficiency and usefulness  

---

## 3. Ease of Use

**Quality Goal**  
Using and achieving the wanted goals with ESBot is easy. The usage itself does not require any mental effort.

**ISO 25010 Classification**  
- Usability: Operability  
- Usability: User Error Protection  

**Measurable Quality Criteria**  
- System Usability Scale (SUS) score of at least 80  

**Verification Method**  
- SUS questionnaire after usage  

---

## 4. Correctness

**Quality Goal**  
All results provided by ESBot are correct. The results follow the content of the users learning material and trustworthy literature.

**ISO 25010 Classification**  
- Functional Suitability: Functional Correctness  
- Reliability: Maturity  

**Measurable Quality Criteria**  
- At least 95% of responses are correct based on verified sources  
- Critical errors occur in no more than 2% of responses  

**Verification Method**  
- Experts check a sample of chatbot answers and compare them with lecture material and literature  
- Responses are reviewed and counted as correct, minor error, or critical error

---

## 5. Controllability

**Quality Goal**  
ESBot always reacts deterministicly and consistently.

**ISO 25010 Classification**  
- Usability: Operability  
- Reliability: Recoverability  

**Measurable Quality Criteria**  
- At least 99% of identical inputs produce consistent outputs under the same conditions  
- No unintended loss of user data (chat history)  
- At least 95% of sessions are correctly restored after navigation actions  

**Verification Method**  
- Repeat the same inputs multiple times and compare the outputs  
- Test navigation actions like reload or back to ensure chat history is preserved  
- Heuristic evaluation (user control and freedom)


_ChatGPT  was used to support with formulating the quality criterion and the verification method_
