# Retrospective on the Review

## 1. Review Process: Good and difficult aspects

### Good
- The choosen review type was good and was a good fit for the team for the first formal review.
- The agreement on the review type and the splitting of review tasks worked well.

### Difficult
- It was quiet difficult to find suitable scenarios / checklists that matched the project scope and are applicable for the reviewed project at the current state.
- It was also difficult to do a manual, formal review as laymen and as non-software developers, because we had no experience yet with reviewing a software project.
- Another difficult thing was that the software projects have not much implementation yet. From our point of view it would be easier to review the software implementation instead of only documentation.


## 2. Suitability of Reviews
- Reviews like peer reviews are suitable for our project team, because you can find important issues.
- More formal reviews like technical review or inspection fit not well for our current experience, because it takes too much time and the process is neither efficient nor worth the effort.
- We would use peer reviews or maybe even kind of technical reviews for specification checking again, because they are more formal and structured. For reviewing code peer reviews through Pull Requests are our choice.


## 3. Improvement for Future Reviews
- For future review rounds we would use better scenarios / checklists that fit better to the reviewed project and its current state.
